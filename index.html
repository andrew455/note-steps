Note Steps

Read the note on the staff, then play it on your piano or keyboard. Your phone listens and tells you if you got it right. There's no timer, so take as long as you need.

Level 1 · C major, right hand

    Finding middle C: look for a group of two black keys near the middle of your keyboard. The white key just to the left of them is C. On the staff, middle C sits on its own short line just below the five lines.
